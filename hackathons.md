### Hack Dearborn: Conjure Reality
Oct 3–4, 2026 · Dearborn, MI

**RobBoss** — "your desk is Bob Ross." A spatial painting coach that uses Gemini for visual reasoning and an HP Sprout for projection, with the closed loop of analyze → break down → guide → paint → observe → adapt.

1st Overall and Best Use of Gemini API. Attended by Nitin, not by Chuka, who was at MHacks 2026 the same weekend. [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/robboss)

### Cal Hacks 13.0
Oct 23–25, 2026 · San Francisco, CA

**Ender's Bench** — puts AI agents to the test: watch their choices, measure their results, and see whether they do the right thing when it matters.

Won Best Use of Spacetime. [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/ender-s-bench)

### Hack The Nest
Nov 14–15, 2026 · Ypsilanti, MI

**Provenance** — cryptographic assurance that a video feed came from a physical capture device. Camera frames are hashed into a chain seeded by an RFID tag's secret, with each segment committed back to the card inside a ~1.1 second window. Built with Nitin.

2nd Overall. The original EEG memory-layer idea was abandoned after roughly 18 hours when the PiEEG hardware could not be made to work; Provenance was built in about 6 hours. [Blog](https://chukaze.dev/blog/prov/) · [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/neurogrep)
